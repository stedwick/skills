## How the judge's notes moved one direction (Portland Print Series)

- **Draft 1 (6.3).** Strongest: their own scene re-inked as a matched print series, with light moving from morning to night. Gaps: one dark wall from top to bottom, so the page did not develop; every room was "poster plus cream label".
- **Draft 2 (6.6).** The builder kept the print series and added a night print where the visitor's light switches on Portland. Gaps: a hard cut at the night print; muddy crops of screenshots.
- **Draft 3 (7.0).** The gallery wall turns into the night sky and Mt Hood rises out of it, one continuous scene. Gap left: the middle rooms still repeat one pattern.

The lesson: each draft kept the strongest thing, fixed the named gaps, and added one new strong thing.
