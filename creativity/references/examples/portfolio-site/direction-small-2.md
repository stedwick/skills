# Direction A2. Small (replacement): "Right Now in Portland"

Why it exists: the first small direction ("One Portland Day") finished at 6.4, below the skill's target of 8, so the skill replaces it once with a different premise of the same size.

- **Premise:** The same page, made live: it shows Alex's world as it is right now in Portland (their real local time and light), and it proves their skills with a dense, beautifully ordered live board of the things he has made, instead of a list of claims.
- **Size:** small. Keep the original's structure, section order, content and dark look; keep the hero art. Add a few strong moves; replace template parts; do not redesign the whole page.
- **The moves:**
  1. **Portland time in the hero.** The hero scene is lit for the current hour in Portland (America/Los_Angeles), computed once on load from the visitor's clock (no API): dawn, day, golden hour, dusk or night, with their own transparent layers re-lit by CSS (night: deep navy sky, gold windows, stars, a soft moon). A small, well-set line under their name says the truth of the moment, in their voice, e.g. "It's 7:42 PM in Portland. Alex is probably walking Biscuit." (choose lines from true facts only: work hours at Cedar Apps, walking Biscuit, cards with friends, asleep). A tiny control lets the visitor scrub the hour to see the other lights (the only time-change interaction; no image swaps).
  2. **A "now" strip** (Jhey Tompkins / henry.codes style) with the facts a recruiter needs: Lead Software Engineer at Cedar Apps · 18 years · Portland, local time · résumé · email.
  3. **"Alex's App Store" becomes a status board.** Dense, beautifully ordered: a departure-board or system-status table of their 11 projects, each row with name, platform, what it is, year or stack where true, a status word ("Live", "Open source", "On the Microsoft Store", "On Gumroad") and a working link. Only true information. The two pixel games get a highlighted row with their pixel logos.
  4. **Skills told as evidence, not chips.** Skills are listed beside where they were used (e.g. "Ruby on Rails: Gradbook, 8 apps, AWS cluster; Fernline API"), drawn only from the original text, in a compact two-column ledger.
  5. **Biscuit walks the footer.** The footer is a strip of their own scene at the current Portland light, with a small, carefully drawn pixel-art Biscuit walking along the river path (CSS animation that stops after a few loops or on reduced motion), and a real link footer on top.
  6. **Template parts replaced:** no stock Icons8 chips, no Bootstrap blue buttons; buttons and links in a palette taken from the hero's current light.
- **Spark:** lens "tell it backwards: start with the ending" (the page opens on right now and the career reads from now back to 2007); form "dense with real information, beautifully ordered" (the board, the ledger, the now strip); domain "a radio drama script" (the hero line reads like a stage direction: "7:42 PM. Portland. A dog waits by the door.").
- **Fingerprint:** palette from the hero at the current hour (day: sky #4FB8E8, snow #F5F8FA, pine #1E3B2A; night: navy #0E1A33, window gold #FFC857, moon #F3EBD0), page stays dark #0F170A; type: a confident serif for headings (Fraunces or Young Serif), a clear sans for text (Atkinson Hyperlegible, 18 px+), a tabular mono only inside the status board; layout: the original column, with the board and ledger as wide, dense tables; image: their own layers re-lit; motion: almost none (Biscuit's walk, the scrub); interaction: hour scrub, board rows.
- **Learns from:** Jhey Tompkins and henry.codes (live "now" line), dimden (status board as proof), localghost and Canvas Cycle (light by clock inside one picture), Mangham (facts strip), samwho (a dog character).
- **Risk:** a status board can look like a corporate dashboard; it must feel handmade and warm (their voice in the status words). The time line must never state something untrue.

All directions must obey: static HTML, CSS and a little JS; assets relative (reuse `../../original/parallax/` and `../../original/legacy/`); no audio; no WebGL; nothing animates forever; reduced motion respected; all true facts kept, none invented; contact details, résumé PDF and project links work.
