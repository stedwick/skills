# Direction C. Large: "Legal Tender"

- **Premise:** Alex's whole résumé is a set of security-printed paper objects you can pick up from a dark table: an ID card with their facts, a ticket for every job, a banknote for a card-game prize, a diploma for each degree, stamps for their side projects, all engraved with Mt Hood.
- **Keep:** the content, facts, their voice, Mt Hood and Portland (as the engraved vignette on every object).
- **Change:** the whole form.
  - The page is night: a deep blue-black table (with a soft lamp pool of light), and on it lie paper objects in cream, green, vermilion and blue inks, with guilloche borders, microprint, serial numbers and fine print that tells true stories.
  - First screen: the **Alex Morgan ID card** (photo slot filled with the engraved mountain, name, "Lead Software Engineer", Portland, 18 years, email, phone, résumé PDF): all recruiter facts in five seconds.
  - Career: **admission tickets**, one per job ("Admit one · Cedar Apps · May 2025 to present · Lead Software Engineer"), with stubs; tap to flip and read the back.
  - Side projects: a sheet of **postage stamps** (11, perforated), each with its name, a tiny line-engraved icon and a working link.
  - Hobbies: a **banknote** for a card-game prize ("Bank of Small Wins"), a ticket stub with a video's view count, a chess rating certificate, and a dog licence for Biscuit.
  - Education: a two-university **diploma** sheet with the two publication titles in fine print.
  - Contact and footer: a **receipt** printed at the bottom with the contact form, then a real footer row of links.
- **Fingerprint:** palette night table #0B1020 with lamp light; paper cream #F3EBD7 (objects only, never the page); banknote green #1F5A47; vermilion #E1452D; ultramarine #2E4BC6; gold foil #D4A93A; type: an engraved display face (Cinzel or IM Fell English SC) for denominations and titles, a sturdy serif for text (Spectral), small mono only for serials; layout: objects overlapping and slightly rotated on the table, at very different scales; image: SVG guilloche patterns and an engraved line version of their own Mt Hood layer (SVG filter or line mask); motion: objects slide in and settle with a spring, flip on tap; interaction: pick up and flip tickets and notes.
- **Learns from:** Kings Wild (true numbers as tickets and notes, fine print as story), Spencer Chang (objects you touch), tonsky (night as a toy), Mangham (facts in one place), Josh Comeau (springs).
- **Risk:** cream paper could read as beige (it must sit on a dark, rich table with strong inks); too many objects can become clutter; flipping must not hide facts.
- **Spark:** 3 (a physical object you can hold; night as the main material) and 2 (typography as image).


All three directions must obey: static HTML, CSS and a little JS; assets relative (reuse `../../original/parallax/` and `../../original/legacy/`); no audio; no WebGL; nothing animates forever except slow, cheap CSS (window twinkle); reduced motion respected; all true facts kept; contact details, résumé PDF and project links work.
