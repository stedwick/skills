# Media reference

For each medium: where to search for the giants (a start, not a limit; small, independent creators only, never large companies or corporate brands), the sensory-craft areas the rubric must cover, the rows of the form fingerprint, and the known machine tells. If the medium is not here, build the same four parts yourself in step 1 and add them to this file afterwards.

## Contents

- Web pages and interactive sites
- Visual design, brand, poster, illustration
- Game levels and game design
- Short fiction and other writing
- Video, film, animation
- Music and sound
- Names, concepts, product ideas

## Web pages and interactive sites

- **Sources (small, independent creators only):** personal sites and portfolios on Awwwards (Portfolio category, Honors), FWA, CSS Design Awards, Hoverstat.es, One Page Love (personal and indie app genres), personalsit.es, Neocities, "Show HN" threads, Codepen and Codrops creators, indie iOS developers' app sites, Neal.fun, explorable essays (Nicky Case, Bartosz Ciechanowski), creative coders such as Lynn Fisher, Bruno Simon, Josh Comeau, Rauno Freiberg, and small studios' own sites. Search "most creative personal websites", "best portfolio 2026", "indie app landing page". The user's references. Skip corporate brand and SaaS sites.
- **Craft areas:** colour, typography (real typefaces, scale contrast), imagery (photography, generated images, 3D, illustration), composition and layout, motion and scroll choreography, interaction (something the visitor can touch or play), voice and copy, proof (real product, real data), performance and phone layout, impact.
- **Fingerprint rows:** palette (actual colours), type families, layout system (grid, full-bleed scenes, horizontal, canvas, editorial), image method, motion idea, interaction idea.
- **Machine tells:** muted off-white, beige or charcoal page with small mono labels and condensed uppercase headings; centred hero with a gradient blob; purple-blue gradient; three feature cards with icons; stock people; "Seamless", "Supercharge", "Unlock"; fake testimonials; giant single word on empty page; crude SVG illustration; everything fades up on scroll; no real product shown. See `calibration/web/`.

## Visual design, brand, poster, illustration

- **Sources (independent artists and small studios):** Type Directors Club and Tokyo TDC student and individual winners, It's Nice That, Society of Illustrators, AOI World Illustration Awards, independent type foundries, Behance and Are.na collections of individuals, zine fairs, gig and record-sleeve posters, AIGA 50 Books | 50 Covers (small presses).
- **Craft areas:** concept, colour, typography, image making, composition, material and texture, system (how it extends), impact.
- **Fingerprint rows:** palette, type, image method, composition principle, material or texture.
- **Machine tells:** symmetrical centred layout, generic sans with gradient, glossy AI-render look, too many elements, soft drop shadows, illustration with no point of view.

## Game levels and game design

- **Sources (indie games first):** IGF winners and finalists, itch.io top-rated and game-jam winners, IndieCade, Day of the Devs, GDC Vault talks by indie designers, the Level Design Book, Game Maker's Toolkit and Noclip pieces on indie games, designers' own blogs and postmortems, RPS and Edge writing on indie games. Study maps, footage frames and the designers' own words.
- **Craft areas:** core idea of the level, teaching without words (introduce, develop, twist, conclude), space and landmarks, pacing and rhythm of tension and rest, player choice, visual readability, secrets and reward, the memorable moment, impact.
- **Fingerprint rows:** core mechanic, space (shape, scale, verticality), pacing, camera or view, tone.
- **Machine tells:** corridor of combat rooms, key-and-door loop, tutorial text boxes, symmetric arenas, no landmark, no moment anyone would retell.

## Short fiction and other writing

- **Sources:** O. Henry Prize, Pushcart, BBC National Short Story Award, Caine Prize, Sunday Times Short Story Award, Best American Short Stories, The New Yorker fiction, Granta, Paris Review interviews, critics' reviews. Quote only short excerpts.
- **Craft areas:** voice, image and concrete detail, rhythm of sentences, structure and what is left out, character desire, the turn, the last line, impact.
- **Fingerprint rows:** point of view, tense, form (letters, list, second person, fragments), voice, sentence length, time span.
- **Machine tells:** "a testament to", "tapestry", "whispered", characters named Elara, tidy moral ending, every sentence the same length, weather opening, explained feelings, triplets of adjectives.

## Video, film, animation

- **Sources (independent makers):** Annecy and Ottawa short films, Vimeo Staff Picks, independent music videos, Art of the Title (indie films), Sundance and festival shorts, Motionographer pieces by small studios and individuals.
- **Craft areas:** concept, image and lighting, editing rhythm, sound, typography in motion, performance, ending, impact.
- **Fingerprint rows:** visual method (live action, cel, 3D, collage, type), editing rhythm, colour, sound idea, length.
- **Machine tells:** stock footage montage, swelling generic music, slow zooms, kinetic text that repeats the voice-over.

## Music and sound

- **Sources (independent artists):** Bandcamp Daily, Mercury Prize shortlists, The Wire and Pitchfork lists of independent releases, Song Exploder, bedroom producers' breakdowns, indie game scores.
- **Craft areas:** motif, harmony, rhythm and groove, sound design and timbre, arrangement, dynamics and structure, production, impact.
- **Fingerprint rows:** instrumentation, tempo and metre, harmonic language, structure, production texture.
- **Machine tells:** four-chord loop, stock risers, no dynamic arc, every element at the same level.

## Names, concepts, product ideas

- **Sources (independent makers):** names of indie apps, games, bands, small shops and one-person products that people love; Product Hunt indie makers; inventors' and founders' own stories; small-press book titles.
- **Craft areas:** clarity, memorability, sound in the mouth, story behind it, fit with the audience, distinctiveness in its market, legal and linguistic sanity, impact.
- **Fingerprint rows:** naming type (real word, compound, invented, metaphor, person), sound, length, tone.
- **Machine tells:** "-ify", "-ly", "Nexus", "Lumina", "Nova", "Sync", Greek god names, two stacked nouns.
