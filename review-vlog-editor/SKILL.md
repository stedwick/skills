# Review vlog editor

First read the shared [vlog editor](../vlog-editor/SKILL.md). Apply this skill to subject reviews, including a talking-head book review with a separate microphone; do not assume an OBS gameplay track, facecam bubble, game audio, or a required outro card.

## Shape the review

- Examine the original recording and transcript. If requested, make a cohesive editable script before a rough cut, with stable paragraph IDs and original source times. Supply the raw uncut transcript separately. Preserve his opinions, humor, qualifications, and engaging observations; remove stutters and false starts without inventing speech.
- Follow his requested length, section order, spoiler boundary, and exact opening/ending. For a book review, a useful starting structure is an intro, title, premise/mystery, setting and characters, themes, strengths and weaknesses, and verdict—but change or combine sections to suit the material and his direction. Place discussion of the mystery's payoff with the mystery when that is the intended structure. Restore useful omitted material in a natural place without introducing the same subject twice.
- Keep transitions and cut speech as full phrases or continuous takes. Never use tiny audio patches to fabricate a clipped word. Use the waveform and inspect actual output edit boundaries as described in the parent skill. If no audio listening capability exists, do not claim subjective audio quality; provide focused previews for Philip to hear.
- Verify factual claims and the actual source text before displaying them. Present a paraphrase as a summary, not as an exact quotation; follow applicable quotation limits. Correct misspelled names. Respect the video's chosen spoiler scope.

## Review visuals

- Use the selected title design and music literally when asked. If he selects a Canva original, preserve its design and do not add his likeness unless requested. Where he asks for an animated version, animate the selected design's components using Canva when available; show a preview. Seek fitting music with the authorized provider and use his chosen cue.
- Add image inserts only where they help explain an actual reference in the narration. Match the subject and timing, animate smoothly, inspect the entrance and exit, and check for leftover flashes after cuts or insertions. Retain the source and any known rights limits for later review. Never present an unverified screenshot as an original or licensed asset.
- If he asks for section graphics, use the selected style consistently, such as animated text over the footage instead of full-screen chapter cards. Follow his ordering and content changes; a spoken point and its section marker move together. Present mock stills before building a new animation if requested.
- A progress strip is optional, not a default. If requested, plan its whole duration before rendering: chapter positions reflect the spoken structure, characters mix idles and short movement, and timing follows his latest direction. Preserve the approved art and poses. For the Caves review he chose option 8 pen-and-ink, occasional progress within each chapter, figures passing in front of the sign, and a black gradient through the bottom edge; those are project choices, not defaults for unrelated reviews. When he names a character-animation skill, read and apply it. Inspect ordered motion and loop transitions, not a still alone.
- Redraw text and lines at delivery size and render figures from the highest-resolution original drawings. A 4K container with an enlarged low-resolution strip is not a high-resolution overlay. Inspect the actual 4K output and provide a motion preview; retain exact accepted visual and audio choices.
